Before you start

Frequently asked questions

What is an Americano tournament?

A format where partners change every round and the standings are individual: each player accumulates the points from the matches they play.

How is it different from Mexicano?

In Mexicano, later pairings depend on the current standings, so players of a similar level face each other.

Do I need a connection at the club?

Tournament management works offline. A connection is needed to download the app, make purchases or send content.

Is it the same app as Padel Americano e Mexicano?

Yes. Padel Match Manager is the project published on the stores also under the name Padel Americano e Mexicano.
