Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This policy explains how the FlipEven mobile application handles and protects user privacy in full compliance with GDPR (EU Regulation 2016/679) and Apple App Store / Google Play Store requirements.

1. Data Controller

The data controller is Kharonte Studio. For any inquiries, feedback, or privacy-related requests, please contact: kharonte.appdev@gmail.com.

2. Core Principle: Total Privacy & Offline Operation

FlipEven is built from the ground up with a strict Privacy by Design philosophy:

  • No Account Required: FlipEven does not require user registration, email sign-ups, or login credentials.
  • No Proprietary Cloud Servers: We do not operate remote databases or cloud servers to collect or process your calculations.
  • Local Data Storage: All inventory records, purchase costs, sales prices, platform fees, shipping costs, and calculated net margins reside exclusively in your device's secure local sandbox.
  • Zero Tracking & Zero Profiling: We do not include third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics trackers, or user profiling tools.

3. In-App Purchases (FlipEven Pro)

Subscriptions and one-time Lifetime purchases are processed securely and directly by Apple App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). FlipEven never sees, handles, or stores your credit card details or financial credentials. We only receive an anonymous cryptographic receipt that unlocks Pro features locally on your device.

4. Photos and Camera Access

Access to the camera or photo library is only requested if you choose to attach photos to items in your inventory. Photos are stored strictly within the app's local private sandbox directory and are deleted when the corresponding inventory item is removed. No photos are ever uploaded or transmitted externally.

5. Data Export, Backup, and Deletion

Exported files (CSV sheets, backups) are generated entirely on your device and shared only through actions you explicitly choose (e.g. Save to Files, AirDrop, or Email). Uninstalling the application completely deletes all locally stored data from your device.

6. Contact and Support

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy:

Support email: kharonte.appdev@gmail.com
