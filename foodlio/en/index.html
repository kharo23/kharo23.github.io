BEFORE YOU START

Frequently asked questions

Who is Foodlio built for?

Restaurants, pizzerias, cafes, catering companies, bakeries, and anyone looking to master their recipe costs.

Does margin equal net profit?

No. The difference between price and ingredient cost does not include staff wages, rent, energy, taxes, or general overhead.

Can I try Foodlio before upgrading to Pro?

Yes, you can start for free with your initial recipes and ingredients. Upgrade options are clearly shown inside the app.
