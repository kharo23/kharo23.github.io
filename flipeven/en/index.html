Frequently asked questions

Who is FlipEven built for?

Anyone reselling clothes, sneakers, collectibles, electronics, or vintage goods on Vinted, eBay, Wallapop, and similar marketplaces.

How are marketplace fees calculated?

You can adjust flat fees and percentage cuts freely or use standard profiles matching each marketplace you sell on.

Are my financial numbers private?

Yes, completely. FlipEven operates 100% offline-first. No financial numbers are ever sent to external cloud servers.
