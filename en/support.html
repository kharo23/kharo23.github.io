Quick Guide & Frequently Asked Questions

If you purchased the Premium version and changed devices (or reinstalled using the same Apple ID or Google account):

  1. Open Preventivi Facili.
  2. Go to Settings or tap the Unlock Premium banner.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Restore Purchases".
  4. The app will verify your receipt with the store and immediately unlock all features with zero extra charge.

From Settings → Data & Backup, tap "Export Archive" to save a backup file to your device or iCloud / Google Drive. On your new phone, select "Restore from File".

Navigate to Settings → Company Details and tap the logo area to select an image (PNG or JPG) from your photo library. Your logo will be cleanly positioned at the top left of all generated PDF documents.
