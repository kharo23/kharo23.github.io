Frequently asked questions

Can I use it for several clients and job sites?

Yes. The client address book keeps your regular clients' details and you recall them with one tap when creating a new quote.

Is my clients' data safe?

The app works with local-only storage on your phone: no external server collects your clients' names or your price lists.

How much does it cost?

You can download it for free and create your first 3 quotes. To continue there is a one-time lifetime purchase with no monthly fees; the current price is shown in the store.

Is there a page for other trades?

Yes, there is also a page for plumbers; it is the same app and works for tradespeople and freelancers.
