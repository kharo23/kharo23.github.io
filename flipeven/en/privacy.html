Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

This policy explains how the FlipEven mobile application handles and protects user privacy in full compliance with GDPR (EU Regulation 2016/679) and Apple App Store / Google Play Store requirements.

1. Data Controller

The data controller is Davide (@kharonteAppDev), independent software developer and author of Kharonte Studio. For any inquiries, feedback, or privacy-related requests, please contact: kharonte.appdev@gmail.com.

2. Core Principle: your data stays on your device

FlipEven is built from the ground up with a strict Privacy by Design philosophy:

  • No Account Required: FlipEven does not require user registration, email sign-ups, or login credentials.
  • No Proprietary Cloud Servers: We do not operate remote databases or cloud servers to collect or process your calculations.
  • Local Data Storage: All inventory records, purchase costs, sales prices, platform fees, shipping costs, and calculated net margins reside exclusively in your device's secure local sandbox.
  • No ads and no profiling: we do not include advertising SDKs, we do not use the advertising identifier and we do not use data for profiling; the content of your inventory is not sent to anyone. To improve the app we collect usage statistics without any content, enabled by default and possible to turn off (section 4).

3. In-App Purchases (FlipEven Pro)

Subscriptions and one-time Lifetime purchases are processed securely and directly by Apple App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). FlipEven never sees, handles, or stores your credit card details or financial credentials. We only receive an anonymous cryptographic receipt that unlocks Pro features locally on your device. To validate receipts and restore purchases we use the RevenueCat service, which only processes anonymous identifiers generated by the store and does not receive your inventory data.

4. Usage Statistics (Google Firebase Analytics)

In published versions the App collects usage statistics, enabled by default: screens opened, features used (items created, sales recorded, quick calculator, exports and backups, photos added, language changes), reaching the free limit, the subscription screen and the purchase outcome (started, completed, canceled, failed and the reason), purchase restores, the category of errors shown and when the rating request appears. Creation actions are recorded only as count bands (1, 2, 3, 4 or more). We do not send names, contact details, business data, amounts or the content of items, prices, costs, margins or photos.

Firebase attaches to these events an identifier generated for each installation of the App (not linked to an account) and standard technical data: app version, device model, operating system, language and approximate region. The advertising identifier is not collected and the data is not used for advertising or profiling.

Legal basis and purpose: the Controller's legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in measuring how the App is used and improving it. The provider is Google (Firebase Analytics); transfers outside the EU rely on the safeguards set out in Google's terms. More details: Google Privacy Policy.

How to turn it off: at any time from the “More” screen, using the “Usage statistics” switch. Collection stops from that moment; data already sent stays in Google's systems for the configured retention period. The switch only covers Firebase Analytics, not the services described in the sections on purchases and diagnostics.

5. Photos and Camera Access

Access to the camera or photo library is only requested if you choose to attach photos to items in your inventory. Photos are stored strictly within the app's local private sandbox directory and are deleted when the corresponding inventory item is removed. No photos are ever uploaded or transmitted externally.

6. Data Export, Backup, and Deletion

Exported files (CSV sheets, backups) are generated entirely on your device and shared only through actions you explicitly choose (e.g. Save to Files, AirDrop, or Email). Uninstalling the application completely deletes all locally stored data from your device.
