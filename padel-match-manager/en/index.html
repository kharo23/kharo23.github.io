BEFORE YOU START

Frequently asked questions

Is this the same app as Padel Americano & Mexicano?

Yes. Padel Match Manager is published on the app stores under the name Padel Americano & Mexicano.

Do I need internet connectivity at the club?

Tournament management works 100% offline. An internet connection is only needed when downloading the app, making purchases, or sharing files.

Which tournament formats can I host?

Americano, Mexicano, Round Robin leagues, and single/double elimination brackets. Mixed and team formats are also supported in the app.
