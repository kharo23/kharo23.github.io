What I build

Three areas, one person in charge.

Native mobile apps

iOS and Android apps built to be fast and to work offline too, keeping data on the device where it makes sense.

Full-stack web platforms

Responsive, accessible front ends and back ends with secure APIs and databases, designed to grow with the product.

Practical AI

Language models, computer vision and semantic processing woven into real workflows, on-device or in the cloud, only where they bring a concrete benefit.

Frequently asked questions

How do we start?

Write to kharonte.appdev@gmail.com with a short description of the project, its users and your deadlines: I'll reply with the next steps.

Do you work alone?

Yes. Kharonte Studio is an independent one-person studio: design, development and release all go through a single contact.

Do you also handle publishing on the app stores?

Yes: build, review, listing, screenshots and support are part of the job, just as for the studio's own apps.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope and complexity: after a first conversation I prepare a proposal for your case.
