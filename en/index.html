Frequently asked questions.

Short answers to what people ask me most.

Who is behind Kharonte Studio?

Kharonte Studio is an independent one-person studio (@kharonteAppDev): I design, build and publish every app, from the interface to the stores.

Which platforms are the apps available on?

Preventivi Facili, Foodlio, Padel Match Manager and Aegis are on the App Store and Google Play. FlipEven is currently being released on the stores.

Do the apps work offline and respect privacy?

They are designed to keep your content on your device and to work offline too: Preventivi Facili stores quotes and clients only on the phone, Padel Match Manager runs tournaments offline, and Aegis has no ads. Some apps collect limited, pseudonymous usage events and diagnostics (for example about purchases or crashes): details are in each app's privacy policy. This site uses no cookies or trackers.

Are the apps free?

The published apps can be downloaded for free. Preventivi Facili offers a one-time lifetime purchase with no recurring fees; Aegis offers monthly and annual subscriptions; for Foodlio and Padel Match Manager the Pro options are shown in the app. Current prices and terms are always listed in the stores.

Do you also build custom software?

Yes. Besides the studio's own apps, I design and build native iOS and Android apps, full-stack web platforms and AI integrations. Write to me at kharonte.appdev@gmail.com with a short description of your project.
